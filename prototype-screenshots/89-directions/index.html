Which visual direction is Settee?

Round two for #89: E combines your reactions to the first four directions, which are still here to compare, all rendered at 1080p from Compose. The catalogue, category counts and artwork are invented, so judge colour, type, highlight and layout, not the paintings. Warm near-blacks still need judging on a real TV.

E's categories sidebar

  • On Live TV, Movies and Shows. It stays tucked away until Left from the leftmost item opens it, on the current category.
  • It pushes the content in one step, with no animation, and Right closes it.
  • "For you" keeps the tab's rows. Any other category opens its whole grid.
  • "Find a category" is only a placeholder, for providers with hundreds of categories.

E's costs

  • No press added on the way to a card, but the rail is now two Lefts from the leftmost item, not one.
  • The Live Preview takes about 140dp of Home, and with it showing, the status text on Favourites sits below the screen edge until you scroll.
  • Text on the art needs a gradient on every card, which is provisional until #84. Real covers also carry their own titles, which may clash with it.
  • The backdrop on Shows and Movies changes on every press and stacks two gradients. It's the costliest part, and provisional until #84.
  • The large header leaves room for about one and a half rows of posters.

E: still to settle

  • Right from the sidebar lands on the nearest row, not the one you left. The real build would need to remember it.
  • The grids here are short samples. A real 8,000-title grid needs a lazy list, which hasn't been measured.
  • Cards are highlighted with a ring and a lift, while the guide and sidebar fill with coral. Check both are obvious from the sofa.
  • Favourite channels have no cover art, so they need their own card if they share Home's Favourites row.

Same in every direction

  • The plain rail with #87's five tabs. Only its colours change, because #90 decides how it behaves.
  • Home's order: Live Preview only after live playback, then Continue Watching, Favourites and new titles.
  • Watch status on every card, and 14sp minimum text.
  • No blur or alpha-dimmed shelves, and a shadow only on the focused item.

Provisional until #84 measures a Stick

  • B: the artwork tint on details.
  • C and D: changing the large image on every press.
  • D: a gradient on every card.
  • All: full-bleed backdrops. A's details and D's Shows stack two gradient layers, and B's details stacks a tint and a gradient. #86 asks for one scrim, so these would need merging.

Trade-offs to weigh

  • C: Favourites is one Right from Continue Watching, so it adds no presses, but its columns go down rather than along.
  • D: no timeline in the guide. Seeing later programmes would need another screen.
  • Amazon recommends cool, less saturated colours. B moves furthest from that.
  • Rows run to the screen edge, so partly hidden text sits outside the overscan margin. The highlighted item always scrolls in, but the margin isn't enforced yet.
