Which highlight can she find from the sofa?

Direction E on the hardest artwork I could make. Each picture is roughly sofa size if it's about 19 cm wide at arm's length (a 43 inch TV at ten feet). Click one for full size. All artwork is generated, and none of this has been on a real TV.

Settled: B, lining figures with the 16 sp floor, and today's gradient on artwork. The notes below are the recommendations as they were made.

The highlight

My pick is C. Coral fill then means "you are here" everywhere, on cards, rows and buttons alike, and it's the only option that holds up on warm artwork without adding a second idea. It's also a flat fill, so it costs no more than the rest. It covers the bottom of the art on the highlighted card, and it's the loudest of the four, so check it still feels calm. B is the runner-up if C feels like too much.

Numbers and text size

My pick is lining figures with the 16 sp floor. Old-style figures make S1 look like SI, and those labels are how she tells episodes apart. It changes what the viewer approved, so it's worth showing them both. In the floor version, everything under 16 sp goes up to 16, Regular text becomes Medium, and synopses go to 18. Nothing in these screens gets cut off mid-letter and the sidebar still fits, but synopses lose a few more words and real titles run longer than these.

Text on artwork

Both are readable. Measured on the white cover, title text is 7.1:1 today and 13.1:1 with the alternative, and quiet text is 6.3:1 and 7.5:1. The alternative keeps more of the picture, because the art stays bright above the text, but the edge of the band is more visible. I'd keep today's gradient, since it's what was approved and it passes.

Neither fixes artwork with its own title printed on it: see Low Tide, where the printed title runs into ours. Only option C's caption covers it, and only while the card is highlighted.

What the renders turned up

  • The large card was being cut off. Lifting it by 1.06 pushed its left edge past its row, so the ring lost a side. All four options here already use the smaller lifts in the table.
  • Text over a bright Shows backdrop fails when the sidebar is open. The gradient stays put while the sidebar pushes the text right, onto the bright part. The category count drops to 1.5:1 and the coral status line to 2.8:1, where 4.5:1 is the usual minimum. The gradient needs to move with the text, whichever option wins. Open Numbers and text size, then Categories, to see it.
  • Coral progress disappears into a coral ring on a highlighted card in A and D. C draws it dark on the caption. B now moves it inside the dark edge and lifts the text clear of it.
  • Down from the large card lands on the second Favourite, not the first, because it goes by position on screen. That's outside this ticket, so I'd carry it to the Home screen work.
