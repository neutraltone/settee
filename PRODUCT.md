# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

android

One APK for Fire TV, Android TV, phones and tablets, written in Kotlin and Jetpack Compose. The Fire TV
Stick is the device it's designed for: a 960 x 540 dp screen seen from ten feet, used with a remote.
Phones and tablets have to keep working, but they don't get a design of their own.

## Users

- **The household viewer (primary).** Someone who isn't technical, watching on a Fire TV Stick from the
  sofa with only the remote. They want to pick up what they were watching, flick through live
  channels, or choose a film, without thinking about providers, streams or settings. The design must
  work for them first. They only get Settee once it reaches 1.0, so don't plan design checkpoints
  around them.
- **The person who sets it up.** They add the provider (usually from a phone, by scanning a QR code),
  pick a guide source, set up Profiles, the parental lock, recordings and device sync. In the household
  this is the owner.
- **Public sideloaders (equal weight).** Strangers who install `settee.apk` from GitHub releases via
  Downloader. They set it up without help, so first run, setup, errors and empty states have to make
  sense to someone who has nobody to ask.

Several people share one TV, each as a Profile, and Settee asks "Who's watching?" when it opens.

## Product Purpose

Settee plays the TV you already pay for. It's a calm, comfortable IPTV player that connects to the
viewer's own provider (Xtream Codes, M3U/M3U8, Stalker portals, Stremio add-ons for films and shows) and
presents live TV, a guide, films, shows, recordings and reminders, and remembers what each viewer has
watched and liked.

Success looks like this: the household viewer reaches what they want within the press limits in
`docs/LOOK.md`, never sees anything technical, and a stranger gets from install to watching without
help.

## Positioning

Calm and comfortable rather than a power-user IPTV box. TiviMate, IPTV Smarters and OTT Navigator
compete on knobs. Settee competes on a warm, quiet room that stays out of the way, built remote-first
for a cheap stick.

Three things a neighbouring product can't honestly copy:

- **No backend, ever.** No account, no subscription, no telemetry, and no server between the viewer and
  their provider.
- **Peer-to-peer household sync.** Watch progress and Favourites move between the household's own
  devices over wifi or Tailscale, or through their own network drive. Provider credentials never sync.
- **Open and checkable.** GPL-3.0, built in the open, with every release APK built by CI from a tagged
  commit.

## Operating Context

- Living room, ten feet away, D-pad remote, often low light. Touch is secondary.
- Low-powered hardware: the Fire TV Stick (about 1 GB RAM, Fire OS 7) is the target, so performance
  budgets constrain every screen (`docs/PERFORMANCE.md`).
- Daily routes: resume Continue Watching, play the next episode, zap live channels, browse the guide.
  Occasional routes: setup, settings, recordings, managing Profiles.
- Setup is done from a phone: the TV shows a QR code and pairing code, and details are typed on the
  phone.
- Providers vary a lot and often misbehave. Failures are routine and must reach the viewer as plain
  sentences (`docs/ERRORS.md`).
- Installed by sideloading. Settee updates itself and says what's new from the changelog.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Home (Continue Watching, Favourites, recently added, Live Preview), Live TV with a guide and
  categories sidebar, Movies and Shows with artwork, details pages with cast via TMDB (Settee's built-in
  key), a player with resume, auto-next episode, subtitles (with OpenSubtitles), catch-up, live pause,
  a sleep timer and PiP.
- Recording to the device, USB or a network drive, with series links. Programme reminders.
- Profiles, a parental PIN lock on categories, and device sync without a server.
- Around 30 interface languages, falling back to English.
- **Constraints:** everything on-device in SQLite. D-pad first: every screen fully navigable by remote
  with an obvious Highlight. Always dark. No Material 3: the UI is built on Compose foundation and
  Settee's own `ui/design` system (ADR 0006). Clean-room: nothing copied or decompiled from another
  IPTV app. Settee supplies no channels or links.
- **Terminology:** `GLOSSARY.md` is the source of names (Provider, Profile, Catalogue Refresh,
  Version, Quality, and so on). Use its terms and avoid the ones it lists under "Avoid".

## Brand Commitments

- **Name:** Settee. The application ID is `uk.neutraltone.settee`. It started as a fork of OpenTV,
  which it still credits.
- **Mark:** a settee that's also a TV, with aerials and a tiny face, plum on a coral tile. It's
  generated by `tools/launcher-art/`. Its uses are set in `docs/LOOK.md` under Identity.
- **Bundled typefaces:** Young Serif 3.003 and Alegreya Sans 2.004, unmodified, under the OFL.
- **Voice:** a calm, capable friend talking to the viewer as "you". British English, sentence case,
  short sentences, contractions. No em dashes, and exclamation marks rarely. Technical terms only in
  setup, with a plain explanation. Errors say what happened, what to do, and whose end the problem is
  on, with no apologies or jokes. Wordplay only on the welcome and setup screens. Settee never says
  "I". `docs/LOOK.md` (Voice) has the full rules and example wording.
- **Never:** a sideloaded IPTV box, a stretched phone app, or a storefront.

## Product Principles

1. **The viewer never waits and never sees the plumbing.** Calm is how it looks, not how fast it goes.
   Daily routes stay within the press limits, and technical detail stays in setup or in an error's
   quiet detail line.
2. **The remote is the interface.** If a design can't be driven easily with a D-pad on a Fire TV
   Stick, it isn't finished.
3. **The viewer's things are sacred.** Favourites, progress, hidden channels and custom names survive
   refreshes, provider changes and devices.
4. **Nothing leaves the house.** No server, account or telemetry. Credentials stay on the device that
   was given them.
5. **Strangers can manage alone.** Every empty state, error and setup step tells someone with nobody
   to ask what to do next.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The requirements in `docs/LOOK.md` cover it: a 16 sp minimum text size (enforced by a test),
contrast ratios recorded for every colour token, the D-pad reaching everything with a visible
Highlight, watch status shown by colour rather than transparency, and an interface in around 30
languages with font fallbacks for scripts Young Serif doesn't cover. No further needs are known.
